    How it works

    1. 01Connect once.

      Add the extension to Chrome, create your account and approve the connection. Then, on your own X profile, one press lets Starling learn how you write.

    2. 02Browse like always.

      Starling adds a ✦ button to posts and to X's composer. Other people's posts you scroll past are not sent anywhere.

    3. 03Drafts appear.

      A reply, a quote, a new post or a rewrite, written in your tone and typed into X's own composer.

    4. 04You press Post.

      Starling fills the composer and steps back. Edit the draft, post it or throw it away. The button is yours. It always was.

    When you ask for a draft or an answer: the post you are answering, the text in the composer, attached images as small copies, your panel messages, your writing samples, and whether you used or changed the draft.

    Your direct messages, your X password and other people's posts you scroll past. Your own profile and post stats are sent only if you turn that on.

    Common questions.

    What is Starling?

    Starling is an AI writing assistant for X, formerly Twitter. It is a Chrome extension with a hosted service behind it: the extension lives inside X, and the drafts are written by Starling's service and its AI provider.

    Does Starling post for me?

    No. Starling puts drafts into X's composer and stops. You decide whether to edit, post or discard them. It never presses Post, likes, follows or sends messages.

    What do I need?

    Desktop Google Chrome, version 120 or later, an X account and a Starling account. The extension does not run on phones; there, use the writing page in your browser and copy each draft into the X app.

    Where does my writing go?

    To Starling's servers and its AI provider, and only when you ask for a draft or an answer: the extension sends the post you are answering, your text and your writing samples. Your direct messages, your X password and other people's posts you scroll past are never sent. The Privacy Policy has the details.

    What counts as a {{ u.name }}?

    A {{ u.name }} is used each time Starling gives you a draft or an answer. If a request fails, even partway through, if you cancel it before anything is shown, or if Starling holds a draft back after its own checks, nothing is used. Allowances reset every month.

    Which topics can it suggest?

    Whatever you post about. Starling learns your niche from your own posts, looks for what is new in it, in news feeds{% if site.web_search is defined and site.web_search %} and web searches{% endif %}, and cites the pages it used. Replies, quotes, rewrites and chat work on any post.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, from your account's billing page. You keep your paid plan until the end of the period you have paid for, then your account moves to Free.

    Is Starling part of X?

    No. Starling is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by X Corp.

    Is this the bank, or the bird?

    Neither. Starling is an AI writing assistant for X. It just shares the name.
