How {{ unit.label }} are counted

  • A {{ unit.name }} is used each time Starling returns a draft or an answer to you.
  • If a request fails, even partway through, or you cancel it before anything is shown, nothing is used. If you stop one after its text has started to appear, it counts.
  • Nothing is used when Starling holds a draft back after its own checks, advises you to wait instead of posting, or finds nothing to improve in your text.
  • Allowances reset every month: on your billing date on a paid plan, and monthly from the day you verified your email on Free.
  • There are no overage charges. When you run out, drafting pauses until your allowance resets or you upgrade.
  • When you upgrade, you pay only the difference for the days left in this period, and get extra {{ unit.label }} in proportion to those days, as soon as the payment goes through.
  • Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
  • If you cancel, you keep your paid plan until the end of the period, then your account moves to Free.

Each plan also has a fair-use limit on the AI processing cost of an account's requests in a period. If an account reaches it, drafting pauses until the allowance resets. It never adds charges.

Payments

{% if ns.currency %}Prices are in {{ ns.currency }}. {% endif %}Payments are processed by Stripe, and card numbers never reach Starling's servers. Paid plans renew automatically each month until you cancel.

If a payment fails, your account page shows what to do. Update your payment method to keep your plan. If the problem is not fixed within a short grace period, the account moves to Free.
